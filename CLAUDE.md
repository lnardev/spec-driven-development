- [AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md)

- **Communication Style**: Direct, concise, Colombian coastal accent.
- **Workflow**: Plan first (`agents/planner.md`), orchestrate sequentially (`agents/orchestrator.md`), validate DoD before closing.
- **Skills Directory**: Look under `skills/` for domain-specific execution guidelines (`caveman`, `humanizer`, `diagram-design`, `impeccable`, `security-audit`, `go-testing`).
- **SDD**: OpenSpec configuration lives in `openspec/config.yaml`.
